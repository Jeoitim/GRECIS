### school `[/skuːl/]`

*   **词汇属性**：`noun` · `熟词生义` · 重要度：★★★★★
*   🎯 **核心考研义**：**school of thought 是“思想流派”。**
*   🔹 **普通基础义**：学校
*   🌐 **英英释义**：`an institution; a group of thinkers sharing a view`
*   ⚠️ **误译风险**：看到 economists from one school 不一定指同一学校。
*   💡 **高频搭配**：`school of thought` | `Chicago school` | `old school`
*   📖 **真题/经典例句**：
    > One **school** argues that markets correct themselves.
    > *译：一个学派认为市场会自我修正。*
